This document details the architectural design, mathematical evaluation metrics,
and processing algorithms used in our 2D-to-3D Monocular Look-Around WebGL Suite.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. CORE ARCHITECTURAL PIPELINE (STAGE-BY-STAGE)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Standard monocular or Side-by-Side (SBS) videos are converted into an interactive
WebGL Look-Around Portal by executing the following backend Python scripts:

STAGE A: INGESTION & DEMUXING (FFmpeg 7.x)
- Apple Spatial Videos (MV-HEVC) contain multiple video layers inside a single HEVC
  stream. We use FFmpeg's modern view specifiers to split the views into discrete
  Left and Right eye streams without quality-loss transcoding:
  `ffmpeg -i input.mov -an -map 0:v:view:0 left.mp4 -map 0:v:view:1 right.mp4`
- Standard packed Side-by-Side (SBS) videos are cropped down the middle to isolate
  the reference monocular left-eye feed.

STAGE B: DENSE GEOMETRIC ESTIMATION (Depth Anything V2)
- Script: `scripts/estimate_depth_video.py`
- Model: Depth-Anything-V2-Small-hf (Transformer-based)
- Role: Estimates dense distance coordinates for every pixel in every frame. 
  Generates a grayscale depth map video stream (`{name}_depth.mp4`). Brighter 
  pixels represent closer objects (inverted metric depth).

STAGE C: FOREGROUND ISOLATION & MATTING (u2net / BiRefNet)
- Script: `scripts/matte_video_rembg.py`
- Model: U2-Net / birefnet-general
- Role: Matting networks predict per-pixel transparency (alpha opacity). This 
  separates moving foreground subjects from complex backgrounds, preventing edge
  halos, static dust, and background "dragging" artifacts. Outputs `{name}_matte.mp4`.

STAGE D: AI OCCLUSION INPAINTING (LaMa)
- Script: `scripts/inpaint_background_lama.py`
- Model: LaMa (Resolution-Weighted Inpainting)
- Role: When the user shifts their head, camera perspective sweeps behind the 3D
  foreground mesh. To prevent black "holes" (occlusions), the matte is expanded 
  (dilated by ~28px) and LaMa AI-fills the missing background plate, producing a 
  clean static/moving backdrop (`{name}_bg_clean.mp4`).

STAGE E: NOVEL VIEWPOINT LIGHT-FIELD SYNTHESIS (DIBR)
- Script: `scripts/synthesize_multiview.py`
- Role: Depth-Image-Based Rendering (DIBR). Warps the reference RGB image across
  a 5x3 (15-camera) virtual lightfield grid from far-left to far-right. Saves
  frames dynamically to support webcam head-tracked viewport blending without
  GPU-intensive rendering overhead.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. PROGRAMMATIC EVALUATION CRITERIA (HOW WE MEASURE QUALITY)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
To audit conversion quality without human visual inspection, the background 
daemon runs a Closed-Loop Computer Vision Feedback Audit in a headless browser:

A. PARALLAX DISPARITY VARIANCE (3D INDEX SCORE)
- The browser places head coordinates at extreme left (x=0.15) and right (x=0.85).
- It takes viewport screenshots and computes the Mean Absolute Difference (MAD):

  V = (1 / (w * h)) * Sum_x Sum_y | I_left(x,y) - I_right(x,y) |

  Where:
  - I_left/I_right are pixel luminance at (x,y) for simulated eye poses.
  - w, h are screenshot dimensions (1280 x 720).

B. THE WARP-TOLERANT CLIPPING SAFEGUARD
- Setting the 3D relief too high causes the WebGL mesh to rip or display jagged 
  vertical "webbing" lines. This artificially inflates the MAD difference score.
- The optimization loop tests multiple relief coordinates ([0.08, 0.16, 0.26]) 
  and chooses the maximum value that keeps the 3D Index strictly below a warp
  ceiling of 10.0, ensuring a clean, physically plausible 3D pop-out.

C. GRADING SCALES
- Spectacular (Score >= 4.0): Distinct horizontal depth volume, clean contours.
- Standard (Score 1.5 to 3.9): Comfortable 3D depth, minor boundary dragging.
- Minimal/Flat (Score < 1.5): No active off-axis displacement (e.g. failed assets).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. LIVE WEBGL RENDERING & VIEWPORT COUPLING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
When loaded, the React + Vite frontend executes interactive WebGL projection:

A. ASYMMETRIC PERSPECTIVE FRUSTUM (Off-Axis Camera)
- MediaPipe or FaceTracker detects front-facing eye/face coordinates (pose.x, pose.y).
- The camera's perspective projection matrix is sheared asymmetrically:
  `camera.projectionMatrix.makePerspective(left, right, top, bottom, near, far)`
-Nearer displaced vertices in the WebGL mesh pop forward, while the background remains
  static, mimicking a real physical look-around window.

B. WEBCAM-FREE REMOTE DESKTOP PARALLAX SIMULATOR
- For Remote Desktop sessions lacking webcam access, the dashboard auto-detects
  localhost and renders a high-precision pose slider. Dragging this slider
  publishes mock head positions (`window.setPoseValue()`) to sweep camera angles
  interactively.

C. INSTANT WEBG-ZOOM & FIT-TO-SCREEN MATRIX SCALING
- Allows the user to instantly adjust the camera crop or zoom from 0.5x to 2.5x.
- Built to handle portrait crops and close-ups (such as the Dancing Demo) by
  multiplying WebGL scale vectors in real-time without refreshing:
  `this.fgMesh.scale.set(z, z, z);`
